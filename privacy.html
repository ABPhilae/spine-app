Privacy policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026.

spine is a personal assistant used by one person, its owner, on his own computer. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it accesses

With the owner's consent through Google's sign-in, spine reads events in his Google Calendar and messages in his Gmail, adds events to his own calendar, and sends mails or saves drafts that he has approved one by one. It never deletes, archives or labels mail, and never invites anyone to an event.

Where the data stays

Data from Google is processed on the owner's own computer. It is not sold, not shared with third parties, and not used for advertising. Parts of it may be sent to an AI model to answer the owner's own questions, only when his privacy rules allow it.

How to stop it

Access can be withdrawn at any time in the Google account: Security, Your connections to third-party apps, spine.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
